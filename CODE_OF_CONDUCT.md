# Code of Conduct

## Our Standards

- Be respectful and constructive
- Assume good intent
- Give and accept feedback gracefully
- Focus on what's best for the project and its users

## Not Acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- Dismissing or belittling contributions
- Trolling or unconstructive criticism

## Enforcement

Violations can be reported by opening a private issue or contacting the maintainer directly. Maintainers reserve the right to remove comments or ban users who violate these standards.

---

*Short version: be a decent human being.*
